## General promotion checklist

Before any historical mechanism becomes canonical in the federation, answer:

1. What external asset or enforceable performing claim appears?
2. What external liability appears or is extinguished?
3. What is the consolidation/legal perimeter?
4. What balanced journal entries prove conservation?
5. What maturity and liquidity fund the position?
6. What happens under default, withdrawal, oracle, bridge and market stress?
7. What evidence status supports execution?
8. What evidence status supports the economic claim?
